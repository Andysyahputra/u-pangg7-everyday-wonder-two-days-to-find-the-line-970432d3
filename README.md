Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3): one sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new.

## The entry

> The toaster popped and for one hot second the kitchen smelled like a house I haven't lived in for thirty years.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). The output is deterministic: the same 111 characters plus a single trailing newline on every run.
